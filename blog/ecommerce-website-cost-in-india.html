What is an eCommerce Website?

An eCommerce website is an online store where customers can browse products, add items to a cart, make secure payments, and place orders. Unlike a regular business website, an eCommerce website includes features such as product catalogue, shopping cart, secure payment gateway, customer accounts, order management, shipping options, inventory management, discount coupons, and order tracking. What Affects eCommerce Website Cost?

There is no one-size-fits-all price. The overall cost depends on your business requirements and the features you need.

Number of Products

A store with 20 products, 100 products, or 1,000 products requires different levels of setup and management. Larger product catalogues involve more time for product uploads, categories, and organization.

Website Design

Your online store should reflect your brand. Businesses can choose ready-made themes, customized layouts, or premium UI/UX design. A custom design provides a more unique shopping experience and strengthens your brand identity.

Payment Gateway Integration

Customers expect secure payment options. Common payment methods include UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Wallets. A reliable payment gateway makes online shopping smooth and secure.

Shipping Integration

Shipping features may include delivery partner integration, shipping charges, order tracking, and delivery status updates. These tools simplify order fulfillment.

Product Management Features

Professional eCommerce websites often include categories, filters, search, product variations, stock availability, and related products. These improve the shopping experience.

Mobile-Friendly Design

Most online shoppers use smartphones. Your website should work perfectly on mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. Responsive design improves usability and supports better Google rankings.

SEO-Friendly Development

An attractive online store is valuable only if customers can find it. SEO-friendly development includes fast loading speed, optimized URLs, product schema, meta titles, meta descriptions, image optimization, and mobile optimization. This helps products appear in Google search results.
