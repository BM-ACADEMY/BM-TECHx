What Affects Social Media Management Pricing?

There isn't a single fixed price because every business has different goals. The pricing depends on several factors.

Number of Social Media Platforms

Managing one platform requires less work than managing multiple platforms — Instagram only, Instagram + Facebook, Instagram + Facebook + LinkedIn, or Instagram + Facebook + YouTube. Each platform requires its own content style and strategy.

Number of Posts and Reels

Content volume significantly affects pricing — weekly posts, daily stories, monthly reels, promotional videos, and educational content. Businesses posting more frequently generally require more planning and creative work.

Graphic Design

Professional graphics help your business stand out. This includes promotional posters, festival creatives, product designs, service highlights, and informative carousels. Strong visual branding improves recognition.

Reel Creation & Video Editing

Short-form videos are among the highest-performing content formats today. Services may include video shoots, editing, subtitles, trend-based reels, voiceovers, and motion graphics. Quality video production often requires additional planning and editing time.

Photography

Original business photos build trust. Professional photography can include products, team members, office, storefront, customer experiences, and behind-the-scenes moments. Authentic visuals usually perform better than generic stock images.

Caption Writing

A good caption does more than describe a post — it can educate customers, build trust, encourage engagement, and drive enquiries. Well-written captions also support better discoverability.

Community Management

Managing comments and direct messages is an important part of social media. Quick responses improve customer satisfaction and encourage more enquiries.

Monthly Reporting

Professional agencies provide reports showing reach, engagement, follower growth, website clicks, profile visits, and best-performing content. This helps businesses understand what is working and where improvements can be made.

Common Social Media Mistakes Businesses Make

Many businesses post regularly but still struggle to see results. Common mistakes include:

  • Posting without a strategy
  • Using poor-quality images
  • Inconsistent branding
  • Ignoring comments and messages
  • Copying competitors
  • Not using Reels
  • No clear call-to-action
  • Focusing only on followers instead of enquiries
  • Posting without understanding customer interests

Avoiding these mistakes can improve your overall social media performance.
